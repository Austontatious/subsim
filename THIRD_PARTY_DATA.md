## Watkins Marine Mammal Sound Database

Some generated hydrophone feature tables, aggregate renderer parameters, evaluation reports, and synthetic renderer configuration under `dataset/hydrophone/`, `data/hydrophone/`, `docs/`, and `reports/hydrophone/` were informed by recordings from the Watkins Marine Mammal Sound Database.

- Required attribution: Watkins Marine Mammal Sound Database, Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution.
- Source recordings are not included in this repository.
- Watkins-informed derived outputs are published for this project's personal, educational, and academic research use only.
- Commercial reuse is not granted by SubSim. Users must obtain any permissions required by the source owner.

See `reports/hydrophone/license_review.md` for the source-by-source engineering review. That review is not legal advice.

## Other hydrophone sources

The hydrophone pipeline also records provenance and license notes for ShipsEar, NOAA, MBARI, Zenodo, and other sources. Raw downloads and normalized source audio remain excluded from Git. Consult each source's current terms before redistributing or commercially using source-derived material.
